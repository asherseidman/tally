Tally Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Tally is a personal desktop planner made by one person for their own use. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and how you can remove it.

1. Summary

  • Your tasks, schedule and settings stay in a database on your own computer.
  • Tally has no server and no account. The developer does not receive your data.
  • Tally uses no advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Google Calendar access is optional, limited to what is listed below, and can be removed at any time.

2. Information stored on your computer

  • Tasks, due dates, time estimates, course names, notes, lists and terms you enter or import.
  • Your work-time availability and scheduling settings.
  • Planned work blocks that Tally creates.
  • Backup copies of this database, kept in a folder you choose (by default a folder in your iCloud Drive). These backups stay in that folder and are handled by your own cloud storage provider under its own terms.

3. Google user data

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Tally asks Google for these permissions (called scopes):

  • See your free and busy times on the calendars you select. Tally receives only whether you are busy during a time range. It does not receive event titles, descriptions, attendees or locations.
  • See the list of your calendars (names only), so you can pick which calendars count as busy time.
  • Create and manage the calendar that Tally itself creates (named "Planner Blocks"). Tally writes study blocks only to that calendar. It does not read, change or delete events in any of your other calendars.

Tally uses this Google data only to work out when you are free and to place and update its own study blocks for you, inside the app. The sign-in credential (a refresh token) is kept in your computer's secure keychain and is sent only to Google. It never goes to the developer or any other party.

4. Limited Use statement

Tally's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Tally does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not allow humans to read it except where you explicitly share it yourself.

5. Sharing

Tally does not share your data with the developer or with third parties. The app contains no artificial-intelligence service and sends nothing to one.

Tally also offers an optional local connector that lets an AI assistant app that you install and run on your own computer (for example the Claude desktop app) read or change your tasks when you ask it to. This works only on your computer, only after you turn it on, and write access is off by default. When you use it, the information you ask about (such as task names and, if you request it, your free time without event titles) becomes part of your conversation with that assistant and is handled under that assistant's own privacy policy. Do not enable it if you do not want that.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Disconnect Google: use Settings, then Google, then Disconnect in Tally, and remove Tally under Third-party apps and services at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Tally stops getting data immediately.
  • Remove the planner calendar: delete the "Planner Blocks" calendar in Google Calendar.
  • Remove local data: delete the app and its data folder on your computer, and delete the backup folder you chose.

7. Children

Tally is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
